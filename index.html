The UV Index is:

      0 to 2 (Low): Minimal risk of harm from sun exposure
      3 to 5 (Moderate): Moderate risk of harm, unprotected skin can start to damage in 30 to 45 minutes.
      6 to 7 (High): High risk of skin and eye damage, skin can burn in 15 to 25 minutes without protection.
      8 to 10 (Very High): Very high risk, skin burns fast. Unprotected skin can burn in 10 to 15 minutes.
      11+ (Extreme): Extreme danger! Skin damage can happen in under 10 minutes, avoid long sun-exposure and cover up!
